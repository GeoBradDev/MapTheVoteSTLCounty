## When you vote

- **Vote if you are in line at 7 p.m.** Everyone in line when the polls close can
  vote.[^rsmo-115-407][^sos-how-to-vote]
- **Vote in private.** No one else may be in the booth or watch how you vote, unless you ask
  for help.[^rsmo-115-445] A parent, grandparent or guardian may bring a child under 18 into
  the booth.[^rsmo-115-445]
- **Get help.** If you cannot read or write, are blind, or have a physical disability, you can get help
  from a person you choose or from two election judges.[^rsmo-115-445] See
  [Voting with a disability, and getting help at the polls](/learn/help-voting).
- **Get instructions.** Two election judges from different parties will explain how to
  vote.[^rsmo-115-445]
- **Get a new ballot.** If you make a mistake, give the ballot back and get a new
  one.[^rsmo-115-439]
- **Vote a provisional ballot.** If there is a question about your registration or ID, you can
  vote a provisional ballot.[^rsmo-115-430][^rsmo-115-427] See
  [Provisional ballots](/learn/provisional-ballots).

## Time off work to vote

You can take up to three hours off work to vote on Election Day.[^rsmo-115-639]

- Ask your employer before Election Day.[^rsmo-115-639]
- Your employer may choose which three hours, while the polls are open.[^rsmo-115-639]
- Your employer may not fire you, punish you or cut your pay for it, if you
  vote.[^rsmo-115-639]
- This does not apply if you already have three hours in a row off work while the polls are
  open.[^rsmo-115-639]

## No campaigning near the door

On Election Day and during absentee voting, people may not campaign or hand out campaign
papers inside the building, or within 25 feet of the door closest to the polling
place.[^rsmo-115-637] The county board can make that distance larger, up to 50 feet, if it posts notice six weeks ahead.[^rsmo-115-637]

It is a crime to interfere with a voter inside a polling place.[^rsmo-115-637]

## If something goes wrong

- Call the St. Louis County Board of Elections at 314-615-1800.[^county-contact]
- You can file a complaint with the Missouri Secretary of State. Call the Elections Complaint
  line at 800-669-8683.[^sos-integrity]
- The complaint form is on the Secretary of State's
  [Elections Integrity page](https://www.sos.mo.gov/elections/elections_integrity).[^sos-integrity]
