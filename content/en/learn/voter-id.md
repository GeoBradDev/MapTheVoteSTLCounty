In Missouri, you must show a photo ID when you vote in person.[^rsmo-115-427] This is true on
Election Day and when you vote absentee in person.[^rsmo-115-427]

## IDs you can use

Bring one of these:

- A Missouri driver's license that is not expired.[^rsmo-115-427]
- A Missouri nondriver's license that is not expired, or that does not expire.[^rsmo-115-427]
- A Missouri driver's or nondriver's license that expired after the last general
  election.[^sos-voter-id]
- A photo ID from the U.S. government or the state of Missouri, such as a U.S. passport.[^rsmo-115-427][^sos-id-flyer]
  It must show your name and photo and have an expiration date.[^rsmo-115-427] The name must
  closely match the name you signed when you registered.[^rsmo-115-427] It must not be
  expired, or it must have expired after the last general election.[^rsmo-115-427]
- A photo ID from the U.S. Armed Forces, the Missouri National Guard, or the U.S. Department
  of Veterans Affairs. It must not be expired, or it must have no expiration date.[^rsmo-115-427]

No other kinds of ID are accepted.[^rsmo-115-427]

## If you do not have one of these IDs

If you are registered, you can still vote a provisional ballot.[^sos-voter-id] It counts if
one of these happens:[^sos-voter-id]

- You come back to your polling place on Election Day, before 7 p.m., with a photo ID from the
  list.[^rsmo-115-427] If you voted absentee in person, you go back to the election office by
  7 p.m. on Election Day instead.[^rsmo-115-427]
- Or the election office checks that the signature on your ballot envelope matches the
  signature on your voter record.[^sos-voter-id]

You get a stub with steps to find out if your provisional ballot was counted.[^sos-voter-id]
See [Provisional ballots](/learn/provisional-ballots).

## Get a free ID for voting

- The Missouri Department of Revenue gives one free nondriver's license to a voter who needs
  a photo ID to vote.[^sos-voter-id][^rsmo-115-427] Call 573-526-8683 to learn more.[^sos-voter-id]
- The state also gives one free copy of papers you may need for that ID, such as a birth
  certificate.[^rsmo-115-427]
- The Secretary of State's office can help you get those papers. Call 866-868-3245 toll
  free.[^sos-voter-id]
