A provisional ballot lets you vote when there is a question about your ID or your
registration.[^rsmo-115-430][^rsmo-115-427] Your ballot goes in a sealed envelope.[^rsmo-115-430] It is
counted after your election board checks that you can vote.[^rsmo-115-430]

## When you might vote a provisional ballot

- You do not have a photo ID from the list.[^rsmo-115-427] See
  [What ID to bring](/learn/voter-id).
- Your name is not in the poll book, and the election judges cannot confirm that you are
  registered.[^rsmo-115-430][^sos-other-questions]

The election judges first try to find out if you are registered, so you can vote a regular
ballot.[^sos-other-questions]

## How it works

1. You fill out the form on the provisional ballot envelope.[^rsmo-115-427] It asks for your name, address,
   date of birth and the last four digits of your Social Security number.[^rsmo-115-427][^rsmo-115-430]
2. You mark your ballot and seal it in the envelope.[^rsmo-115-430]
3. You get a stub from the envelope with steps to check on your ballot.[^sos-voter-id]

## When it counts

If you voted provisionally because you had no photo ID, your ballot counts if:[^rsmo-115-427]

- You come back to the same polling place where you voted, by 7 p.m. on Election Day, with a
  photo ID from the list.[^rsmo-115-427] If you voted absentee in person, you go back to the
  election office by 7 p.m. instead.[^rsmo-115-427]
- Or your election board finds that the signature on your envelope matches the one on your voter
  record.[^rsmo-115-427][^sos-voter-id]

The City Board says no provisional ballots are available at its no excuse absentee sites, so
bring a photo ID to vote early in the City.[^city-no-excuse]

Any provisional ballot counts only if your election board finds that you are registered and
eligible to vote in this election.[^rsmo-115-430]

## Where to vote

### St. Louis County

A registered voter may vote at any polling place in St. Louis County on Election
Day.[^county-polling-places]

### City of St. Louis

Any voter registered at an address in the City can vote at any of the City's 56 listed
polling places on November 3, 2026.[^city-polling-places]

### At the wrong polling place

The County rule above is for St. Louis County voters,[^county-polling-places] and the City
rule is for voters registered at a City address.[^city-polling-places] Under Missouri law, a
provisional ballot cast at the wrong polling place is not counted.[^rsmo-115-430] If the
election judges find you are at the wrong place, they tell you where to go.[^rsmo-115-430] You
can go to a polling place your election board's rule above allows, or a central polling
place.[^rsmo-115-430]

## Find out if your ballot counted

The state has a free way to learn if your ballot counted, and why not if it did
not.[^rsmo-115-430] Your stub tells you how to check.[^sos-voter-id] For a provisional ballot
cast without a photo ID, call 866-868-3245 toll free and give the PIN on your
stub.[^sos-id-flyer] Wait 14 days after the election before you call.[^sos-id-flyer]

If you were not registered, your envelope can be used to register you for future
elections.[^rsmo-115-430]
