## When

Polls are open from **6 a.m. to 7 p.m.** on Tuesday, November 3, 2026.[^rsmo-115-407] If you
are in line at 7 p.m., you can still vote.[^rsmo-115-407][^sos-election-day] If you get there after
the polls close, you cannot vote.[^sos-election-day]

## Where

Check where to vote before Election Day. The county board can tell you your polling place;
checking ahead helps you avoid problems.[^sos-election-day] Call 314-615-1800.[^county-contact]

To find polling places near you, look up your address on the [home page](/) or use the
county's
[Polling Place Lookup](https://stlouiscovotes.maps.arcgis.com/apps/instant/nearby/index.html?appid=13fb36e785c84dbdbcc5219f999652ba).[^county-polls-app]

## Step by step

1. Bring a photo ID. See [What ID to bring](/learn/voter-id).[^sos-how-to-vote]
2. Show your ID to the election judges and sign the poll book.[^sos-how-to-vote]
3. Take your ballot to a voting booth and mark it alone.[^rsmo-115-439][^rsmo-115-445]
4. Mark each race on its own. Missouri has no "straight party" option.[^sos-how-to-vote]
5. Read the instructions posted at the polling place, or ask an election judge to show you how
   the voting equipment works.[^sos-how-to-vote]

## If you make a mistake

- If you mark a ballot wrong or damage it, give it to an election judge and get a new
  one.[^rsmo-115-439]
- If you mark more choices in a race than allowed, you get a chance to fix your
  ballot.[^sos-how-to-vote]

## Good to know

- A parent, grandparent or guardian can bring a child under 18 into the voting booth.[^rsmo-115-445]
- If you need help in the booth, ask. Two election judges from different parties will give
  you instructions.[^rsmo-115-445] See
  [Voting with a disability, and getting help](/learn/help-voting).
- If there is a problem with your registration, you may be able to vote a provisional
  ballot.[^sos-other-questions] See [Provisional ballots](/learn/provisional-ballots).
