## Bring someone to help you

If you cannot read or write, are blind, or have another physical disability, and cannot vote
your ballot on your own, you can bring a person to help you vote.[^sos-election-day][^rsmo-115-445]
Your helper does not have to be 18 or a registered voter.[^sos-election-day]

- You tell the election judges, under oath, why you need help.[^rsmo-115-445]
- Your helper goes into the booth with you and marks your ballot the way you say.[^rsmo-115-445]
- A helper can help only one voter in each election, unless they are an election judge or a
  member of your immediate family.[^rsmo-115-445]

## Get help from election judges

You can ask the election judges for help instead. Two judges from different parties go to the
booth with you and mark your ballot the way you say.[^rsmo-115-445][^sos-election-day]

If you only need instructions, two judges from different parties will explain. Then they leave
so you can vote in private.[^rsmo-115-445]

## Other ways to vote

- You may be able to vote curbside, outside the polling place. Every polling place must have
  accessible voting equipment.[^sos-election-day] See
  [Accessible voting and curbside voting](/learn/accessible-voting).
- You can vote absentee if you are sick or have a physical disability. You can also vote
  absentee if you are the main person who gives physical care to someone at your address who
  is.[^rsmo-115-277] See [Absentee and early in-person voting](/learn/absentee).

## Permanent disability list

If you have a permanent disability, you can ask the county board to put you on its permanent
absentee list.[^rsmo-115-284][^sos-election-day]

- The county board then sends you an absentee ballot application before each election you can
  vote in.[^rsmo-115-284]
- Return the application by 5 p.m. on the second Wednesday before the election to get your
  ballot.[^rsmo-115-284] For November 3, 2026, that deadline was Wednesday, October
  21.[^rsmo-115-284]
- You do not need a notary for your ballot envelope.[^rsmo-115-283]
- The list is kept private.[^rsmo-115-284]

## Help with an absentee ballot

If you are blind, cannot read or write English, or have a physical disability that keeps you
from marking your ballot, a person you choose can help you with your absentee
ballot.[^rsmo-115-291]

## If you become sick or go into a care facility

This applies if, after the request deadline, you:[^rsmo-115-287]

- went into a hospital in St. Louis County,[^rsmo-115-287]
- became confined because of illness or injury, or[^rsmo-115-287]
- will be in a nursing or care facility in St. Louis County on Election Day.[^rsmo-115-287]

The county board sends a team to help you apply and vote. The team is two registered voters,
one from each major party.[^rsmo-115-287] Call 314-615-1800.[^county-contact]
