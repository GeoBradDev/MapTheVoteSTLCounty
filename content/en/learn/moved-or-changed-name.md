Tell the county board when you move or change your name.[^sos-other-questions] It is best to do this before
Election Day.[^sos-other-questions] Call 314-615-1800 with questions.[^county-polls-app]

## You moved within St. Louis County

If you are still on the voter list, you can still vote.[^rsmo-115-165] On Election Day, you may
vote at any polling place in St. Louis County.[^county-polling-places] Update your address
there and vote.[^sos-other-questions][^rsmo-115-165] You tell the election judges your new
address, out loud or in writing.[^rsmo-115-165]

## You moved to St. Louis County from another Missouri county

If you are still registered at your old address, you can update your address on Election Day.[^rsmo-115-165][^sos-other-questions]
Do it in person at the St. Louis County Board of Elections office.[^rsmo-115-165][^sos-other-questions]
You must show a photo ID.[^rsmo-115-165][^rsmo-115-427] See
[What ID to bring](/learn/voter-id).

## You moved to Missouri from another state

You need to fill out a new voter registration form.[^sos-other-questions] The deadline for the
November 3, 2026 election was October 7, 2026.[^sos-calendar]

## You changed your name

- Tell the county board before the election, or tell the election judges at your polling
  place.[^sos-other-questions]
- After you tell the election judges, you can vote one time under your old
  name.[^sos-other-questions][^rsmo-115-167]
- The county board updates your record and sends you a new voter card.[^sos-other-questions]
