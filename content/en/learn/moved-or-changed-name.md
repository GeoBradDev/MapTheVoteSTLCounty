Tell your election board when you move or change your name.[^sos-other-questions] It is best to do this before
Election Day.[^sos-other-questions] In St. Louis County, call 314-615-1800 with
questions.[^county-polls-app] In the City of St. Louis, call 314-622-4336.[^city-update-registration]

## You moved within St. Louis County

If you are still on the voter list, you can still vote.[^rsmo-115-165] On Election Day, you may
vote at any polling place in St. Louis County.[^county-polling-places] Update your
address and vote.[^sos-other-questions][^rsmo-115-165] You tell the election judges your new
address, out loud or in writing.[^rsmo-115-165]

## You moved within the City of St. Louis

If you moved from one City address to another, you can change your address on Election Day at
the polling place for your new address.[^city-update-registration][^rsmo-115-165] Any registered
City voter may also vote at any of the City's polling places.[^city-polling-places] To have
your new address on file before the next election, send the City Board a new voter
registration form by the fourth Wednesday before that election.[^city-update-registration]

## You moved here from another Missouri county

This includes a move between St. Louis County and the City of St. Louis.[^rsmo-115-165] If you
are still registered at your old address, you can update your address on Election
Day.[^rsmo-115-165][^sos-other-questions] Do it in person at your new election board's office:
the St. Louis County Board of Elections office, or the City's Board of Election Commissioners
at 300 N. Tucker Blvd.[^rsmo-115-165][^sos-other-questions][^city-board] You must show a
photo ID.[^rsmo-115-165][^rsmo-115-427] See [What ID to bring](/learn/voter-id).

## You moved to Missouri from another state

You need to fill out a new voter registration form.[^sos-other-questions] The deadline for the
November 3, 2026 election was October 7, 2026.[^sos-calendar]

## You changed your name

- Tell your election board before the election, or tell the election judges at your polling
  place.[^sos-other-questions]
- After you tell the election judges, you can vote one time under your old
  name.[^sos-other-questions][^rsmo-115-167]
- Your election board updates your record and sends you a new voter card.[^sos-other-questions]
- In the City, update your name with a new voter registration form: check "Name Change" in
  box 3 and mail it to the City Board, or register in person.[^city-update-registration]
