## Curbside voting

If you have limited mobility, you can vote "curbside," outside the polling
place.[^sos-how-to-vote]

1. Go to your polling place.[^sos-how-to-vote]
2. Ask someone to go in and ask the poll workers to bring a ballot out to you.[^sos-how-to-vote]
3. Two election judges, one from each major party, bring you a ballot and what you need to
   vote.[^rsmo-115-436] You must be within 200 feet of the polling place.[^rsmo-115-436]
4. You mark the ballot.[^rsmo-115-436] The judges seal it in an envelope and put it in the ballot
   box.[^rsmo-115-436]

The poll workers should bring you a ballot within a reasonable time.[^sos-how-to-vote]

## Accessible voting equipment

Every polling place must have an accessible voting system for voters with
disabilities.[^sos-how-to-vote] It can read the ballot to you out loud, or make the text on
the screen bigger.[^sos-how-to-vote]

## A more accessible polling place

Each local election office must have at least one central polling place that is easy to get
into for voters with physical disabilities and older voters.[^rsmo-115-115]

### St. Louis County

A registered voter may vote at any polling place in St. Louis County on Election
Day.[^county-polling-places] If you have a physical disability, you can also ask the county
board for a polling place that is accessible to you.[^rsmo-115-436] Or you
can vote at a central polling place.[^sos-how-to-vote] Call the county board at 314-615-1800
to ask.[^county-polls-app]

### City of St. Louis

If your polling place is not accessible, you can go to any of the City's polling places,
where any registered City voter may vote.[^city-accessible] City polling places must have
wheelchair-accessible voting booths, doorways at least 32 inches wide, handrails on all
stairs, and voting equipment for voters who are blind or have low vision.[^city-accessible]
Call the City Board at 314-622-4336 with questions.[^city-board]

## Ask for voting materials you can use (City of St. Louis)

The City Board provides audio files, large print and other voting accommodations on
request.[^city-disabled-access] Ask the City Board in person or by phone at 314-622-4336, no
later than two weeks before Election Day.[^city-disabled-access] For November 3, 2026, that
was Tuesday, October 20.[^city-disabled-access] You can also ask for accessible materials
when you apply for an absentee ballot.[^city-accessible]

## If you could not vote because of a disability (City of St. Louis)

You can file a complaint with the City Board, in writing or out loud.[^city-ada-grievance] At
your polling place, ask the election judge for an ADA Complaint Form.[^city-ada-grievance]
File it as soon as you can, within 30 days if possible.[^city-ada-grievance] The City Board's
[grievance page](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/government/departments/board-election-commissioners/voter/ada-grievance.cfm)
has the form and where to send it.[^city-ada-grievance]

## Absentee ballots for voters with a permanent disability

If you have a permanent physical disability, you can ask to be put on a special list.[^sos-how-to-vote] Then
your election board mails you an absentee ballot application before each
election.[^sos-how-to-vote][^city-permanent-disabled] See
[Voting with a disability, and getting help at the polls](/learn/help-voting).
