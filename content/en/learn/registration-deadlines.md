## The deadline for November 3, 2026 has passed

The last day to register to vote in the November 3, 2026 general election was
**Wednesday, October 7, 2026**.[^sos-calendar]

For most voters in Missouri, the deadline is the fourth Wednesday before an election.[^rsmo-115-135] A
mailed form must be postmarked by that day.[^sos-register]

## If you missed it

- If you register now, you can vote in later elections.[^rsmo-115-135]
- If you are already registered and moved, you may still be able to vote. See
  [If you moved or changed your name](/learn/moved-or-changed-name).[^sos-other-questions]

## Military voters who just came home

Some members of the military, and people who just finished a job overseas, can register late.
This is for people who were discharged, came back from deployment, or left a job outside the
United States after the deadline.[^rsmo-115-135] They can register in person at the county
board until 5 p.m. on the Friday before the election.[^rsmo-115-135] They must bring papers
that show they qualify.[^rsmo-115-135] See
[Military and overseas voters](/learn/military-overseas).

## Registering for future elections

You can register online, by mail or in person.[^sos-register] Start on the Secretary of
State's [Register to Vote page](https://www.sos.mo.gov/elections/goVoteMissouri/register).
